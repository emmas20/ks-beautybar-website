Spray tan

A glow made for your skin

Custom spray tan · $55 · 35 min

Spray Tan Prep
  1. Exfoliate 2-3 times prior to appointment with NON MOISTURIZING EXFOLIANT or Exfoliating Mitt with lotion free soap.
  2. Moisturize leading up to the night before (last shower must be completed minimum of 4hrs prior to service)
  3. Shave and exfoliate the night before appointment (use razor that doesn't have moisture strips). If getting waxed make sure it is 2+ days before appointment
  4. NO DEODORANT OR LOTION the day of appointment
  5. Bring a long sleeve shirt and loose, soft pants to appointment for after. THIS IS IMPORTANT for the best result. NO DRESSES
Aftercare
  1. Wear a loose fitted long sleeve shirt and long pants until your post tan shower.
  2. DON'T get wet or sweat
  3. Wait the right amount of time to shower (your service provider will give you a time)
  4. DO NOT scrub or exfoliate during your post tan showers. When drying off, pat dry with towel instead of rub.
  5. Moisturize 2x a day to maintain tan for 7-10 days with low fragrance and low alcohol lotion
  6. If using sunscreen make sure it is zinc based. NO spray sunscreen
