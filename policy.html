Booking policy

You'll be asked to review and agree to this policy before you confirm your appointment.

Card on file

  • A credit or debit card is required to book and is kept securely on file. You will not be charged at the time of booking.
  • Your card will only be charged in the event of a no-show or a cancellation made less than 48 hours before your appointment. You will not be charged otherwise.

Changes and cancellations

  • Please make any changes or cancellations at least 48 hours before your appointment.
  • No-shows and cancellations within 48 hours will result in the card on file being charged.

Existing clients

  • If you are a pre-existing client, a card on file will not be required.
  • However, if you cancel your appointment last-minute or do not show up, the applicable cancellation or no-show fee will still be required to book future appointments.

No-shows and future bookings

  • If you no-show without contacting me, you will no longer be able to book appointments online.
  • After a no-show, future appointments must be requested directly through me rather than booked online, and will require a deposit to secure your booking.

This helps me protect my time, support my small business, and allows me to continue not taking deposits for appointments. Thank you so much for your understanding and ongoing support. Please feel free to reach out with any questions at (403) 872-0440 or ksbeautybark@gmail.com.
